EmbroMaster: Controllable Multi-Stitch Embroidery Synthesis with Craft-Aware Planning

Zhanlin Chen1,*, Zheng Gu2,*, Hao Liang1, Wenhua Shi3,†, Xin Jin4,5, Jing Huo1,†, Yang Gao1

1 State Key Laboratory for Novel Software Technology, Nanjing University 2 College of Computer Science and Software Engineering, Shenzhen University 3 School of Art and Design, Shanghai Institute of Technology 4 Beijing Electronic Science and Technology Institute 5 State Key Laboratory of General Artificial Intelligence, Beijing Institute for General Artificial Intelligence (BIGAI)

* Equal contribution. † Corresponding author.

TLDR: EmbroMaster turns flat designs into controllable multi-stitch embroideries through craft-aware planning, using an editable embroidery blueprint to guide synthesis and support interactive, region-level stitch editing.

Overview

Abstract

Generating embroidery takes more than imitating thread texture: a model must decide which stitch suits each region and render several distinct stitches together in one image. However, current generative models lack the embroidery knowledge to plan which stitch a region should use. Even when specific rules are given, they still struggle to render multiple stitches in one image. In this paper, we present EmbroMaster to address these problems. We first construct StitchAtlas, a multi-stitch embroidery dataset. We collect real embroidery samples and train models on them to expand the dataset with high-quality synthetic images, along with stitch craft knowledge collected from embroidery experts. We then introduce a craft-aware planning agent. It takes a flat design and user constraints as input and produces a structured stitch plan, called an embroidery blueprint, that assigns a stitch to each region. Finally, we develop a multi-stitch synthesis pipeline that follows the blueprint to generate embroidery images and supports interactive, region-level stitch editing. Experiments on six stitch types demonstrate that our method achieves better generation quality than existing methods. Embroidery experts also rate our results higher on stitch assignment appropriateness and visual realism, suggesting the potential of our method for various application scenarios.
